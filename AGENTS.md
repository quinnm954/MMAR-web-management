
- Read subdirectory AGENTS.md before changing files there.
- Admin provider balances are fetched by the admin-only `admin-service-usage` edge function, with independent service results and server-only connector credentials; why: account billing data must not reach technicians or customers and one unavailable service must not hide the other.
- Receptionist offer windows use shared `receptionistSlots` separately from physical `openSlots`; voice tools guard morning requests and availability failures fail closed; why: customer preferences must not change staff calendar hours or permit unchecked times.
- Automated booking and receptionist SMS are tagged `ai` at insertion; why: system replies must not trigger the human-text two-hour suppression window.
- General SMS intake validates offered and accepted slots against checked availability and rechecks before saving structured date/time on new or existing pending requests, never appointments; why: owner approval requires an actionable request without invented or auto-confirmed times.
- Oil-change eligibility is enforced by the service-only `oil_change_eligible` RPC, booking/appointment validation triggers, and shared edge guards before automated time offers; why: a receptionist prompt alone cannot enforce subscriber-only service.

- Reports classify refunds as revenue/profit reductions on completion date, never sales, labor or tech pay; why: refunds must not inflate sales or reverse labor.
- Reports recognize financing cash from paid financing_payments on paid_at (including interest), excluding financed invoice totals and mirrored invoice_payments; receipt rows do not repeat sales, parts or labor costs, because one collection must count once.
- Reports year charts zero-fill elapsed months and leave unrecorded future months null; why: show every month without projections.
- Reports use reportPeriod; lists share dateFilters/DateRangeFilter. Starts are inclusive, ends exclusive, custom end days included, WTD starts Monday; why: consistent historical filtering.

- Fleet registration signs up then calls `submit_fleet_registration`, guarded by user id/email match within 15 minutes; why: secure public signup.

- Financing receipts use service-only `record_financing_stripe_invoice`, keyed by Stripe invoice/date; principal excludes interest, invoices close after all installments clear. Day-before SMS uses the existing worker and Stripe billing/retry times; why: no duplicates, early closing or invented charge times.
- The Admin Phone workspace is the only screen for calls, texts and email; in-app chat (/messages, message_threads) is retired for every role; why: the owner communicates only by SMS, email and calls.
- Repair-order names derive from approved estimate repair lines, preferring labor descriptions; why: orders must identify the repair rather than the estimate workflow state.
- Estimate completion is synchronized by invoice/estimate triggers using explicit invoice links first, then appointment/service-record links only for invoices created after the estimate; why: payment must close estimates without an old diagnosis payment closing newer repairs.
- Technician agreement: `technician_agreements` table (admin-only RLS) signed by techs via token link through `get_technician_agreement`/`sign_technician_agreement` RPCs; text lives in `src/lib/techAgreement.ts`; why: techs sign on phone without an account.
- Website booking requests get an unsent draft estimate at submission (notify-booking-request → booking_requests.draft_estimate_id), which estimate-for-appointment reuses on confirmation; why: the shop sees a price before booking, without auto-sending or duplicating estimates.
- The admin Phone also runs as its own home-screen app at /admin/phone (separate phone.webmanifest swapped in at runtime, fullscreen AdminPhoneHub with left-edge swipe back); why: Mike uses it on his iPhone like a native phone app.
- Web push subscriptions carry an `app` tag ('main' | 'phone'); send-web-push routes `message_updates` to phone-app subscriptions when present (else main) and all other categories to main; why: communication alerts belong in MMAR Phone, service alerts in Garage Ace.
- App-icon badges count only the alerts belonging to that home-screen app (phone app: `message_updates`; main: everything else), refreshed on resume; why: iOS badges are per installed app and only update when it runs or a push arrives.
- Job completion time lives in `appointments.completed_at`, set by trigger when status becomes completed and cleared if reopened; why: dashboards count finished jobs by when they finished, not by later edits.
- Techs finish jobs through the `tech_finish_job` RPC (marks complete, logs mileage, creates the invoice, alerts admins), then `send-invoice-payment-link` lets the assigned tech trigger the send without ever receiving the price or link; why: one tap at job end keeps completion times and invoicing accurate.
- Blocked-off time lives in `calendar_blocks` (admin-only RLS), managed through the admin Calendar's create/edit/remove dialog using Eastern time; new blocks are global because receptionist availability currently spans all regions; open-slot suggestions, the admin calendar and the ICS feed all honor it; why: one source for when the shop is unavailable.
- Service regions live in `service_regions` (active = launched) with `user_service_regions` for techs; a launched region with no tech is waitlist-only (receptionist takes details, no slots/prices); bookings/appointments get `region_slug` by trigger from the service address; South Florida website pages are always indexable while booking stays gated by `SOUTH_FLORIDA_BOOKING_OPEN`; why: launch regions without code changes while never booking work nobody can do.
- The AI receptionist auto-syncs: triggers on phone_settings/service_regions/user_service_regions set `ai_sync_requested_at` and call `ai-receptionist?action=setup&auto=1` (allowed only while a sync is pending); followup-worker retries hourly; why: the owner shouldn't have to press an update button.
- New appointments auto-assign the region's only tech by trigger; with several techs `needs_tech_choice` is set and staff are alerted, never overwriting a manual pick; why: one-tech regions need no dispatch step.
- Regional home pages (/ and /south-florida) share `RegionHome`; sections read `useHomeRegion()` to swap only local copy, and `SOUTH_FLORIDA_BOOKING_OPEN` in `src/lib/regions.ts` hides the website waitlist notes; why: identical design per region without duplicating the page.
- Old URLs Google still lists are mapped to the closest live page by `src/lib/legacyRedirects.ts` (client Navigate plus prerendered canonical/refresh stubs), because Lovable hosting ignores `public/_redirects`; why: keep ranking value from removed cities and services.
- Garage Ace staff shells (admin, tech, phone app) full-reload via useAutoRefresh every 30s and on new notifications, deferred while typing, dialogs, calls or hidden; why: most screens fetch once on open.
