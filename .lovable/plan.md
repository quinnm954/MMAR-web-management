# MMAR Fleet Brochure Plan

## Goal
Create a polished, print-ready tri-fold brochure that sells Mike’s Mobile Auto Repair to both used-car dealerships and commercial fleet operators. The brochure will emphasize faster vehicle turnaround, on-site convenience, documented service history, transparent pricing, and the advantages of choosing an established mobile service partner over a traditional shop or one-person mobile mechanic.

## Six-panel content

### Outside spread
1. **Front cover** — MMAR logo, strong fleet-focused headline, branded vehicle/service imagery, and “5+ vehicles” positioning.
2. **Fold-in panel** — concise comparison showing the MMAR advantage: on-site service, less downtime, written quotes, per-VIN records, warranty-backed work, recurring scheduling, and consolidated billing.
3. **Back panel** — fleet quote call-to-action with phone/text number, service area, website, and a scannable QR code to the fleet page.

### Inside spread
1. **Used-car dealerships** — lot-side diagnostics and repairs, reconditioning support, fewer tow/shop delays, documented work, and faster inventory readiness.
2. **Commercial fleets** — preventive-maintenance scheduling, priority response, mixed-fleet support, per-vehicle records, and simpler account billing.
3. **Pricing and process** — current fleet discount tiers, vehicle-class reference ranges for offered services, fuel-type adjustments, and the four-step VIN-based quote process. Oil changes and pre-purchase inspections will remain excluded from the promoted service offering.

## Visual direction
- Match the existing MMAR identity: near-black foundation, sky blue and golden yellow accents, current MMAR logo, bold automotive display type, and clean commercial typography.
- Use real MMAR service photography where suitable, with disciplined grids, strong contrast, and restrained automotive details rather than generic brochure styling.
- Keep copy brief and scannable while retaining the concrete pricing and operational benefits business buyers need.

## Deliverables
- **Printer-ready PDF:** US Letter landscape tri-fold, two sides, 0.125-inch bleed, safe margins, fold allowances, crop marks, and embedded fonts.
- **Web images:** high-resolution PNGs for the outside and inside spreads, plus individual panel crops for sharing online.
- **Design philosophy:** a short companion markdown file documenting the visual system used.
- Group the finished files together in Files for easy access.

## Quality checks
- Validate the PDF structure and visually inspect both sides after rendering.
- Verify every panel crop, fold-safe placement, QR readability, pricing accuracy, color consistency, margins, and absence of clipping or overlap.
- Refine and rerender until all print and web outputs pass inspection.
